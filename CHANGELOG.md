# Changelog

## Unreleased

- The Marketplace window: markets with a picture, description, level and a folder of merchants; shop cards; each shop's stock by group (weapons, armor and shields, consumables, runes, gear, treasure) with level, rarity, stock and price, and descriptions on a click. Shops sell identified goods: an item the merchant has unidentified shows as the real item.
- Buying: add things to a cart from any open shop (a batch at a time for things sold in batches, never more than the shop has), then buy them for one of your characters or the party stash. The cart shows each shop's lines, the total and what the buyer will have left, and marks anything that's sold out or no longer for sale. The GM's client makes every trade, so a GM must be logged in; it checks the stock, the price and the buyer's money first, takes the coins (making change), puts the items in the buyer's inventory, identified and stacked with what they have, and pays the shop.
- Selling: a shop's Sell tab lists what the chosen character carries, with the shop's offer for each (half an item's price and full value for treasure by default, set per shop). Unidentified items have to be identified first. What a shop buys goes into its stock. The shop pays from its purse, the coins on its merchant sheet, unless the GM gives it an unlimited purse.
- Purses: a new shop starts with one level's party treasure for its level (GM Core's table; 100 gp below level 1), and Fill purse tops any shop up to that. A world setting scales it for poorer or richer regions.
- Shopkeepers and prices: drag an NPC onto a shop to make them its shopkeeper. Their attitude changes what players pay when buying (not what the shop pays them): with PF2e NPC Attitudes, their attitude toward each character, else a setting on the shop. The percent for each attitude is a world setting (Friendly 10% off, Helpful 20% off, Unfriendly 20% more by default), and a hostile shopkeeper won't trade unless the GM turns that off.
- A trade log for the GM: every purchase and sale, who made it, the shop and the money.
- For the GM: new markets and shops, Visible (players can browse) and Open (players can trade), hidden shops, merchants added by dragging them onto a market.
